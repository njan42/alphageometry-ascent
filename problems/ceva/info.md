# Ceva's theorem (interior concurrence implies product of ratios)

AD, BE, CF concur at O inside triangle ABC. Prove BD/DC × CE/EA × AF/FB = 1.
The goal uses log distances; all six lengths are positive. This is the forward,
interior case, not the directed-length converse. Coordinates are a generated
instance, not extra premises.

Source: [Ohio State, The Theorems of Ceva and Menelaus](https://math.osu.edu/sites/math.osu.edu/files/ceva-menelaus.pdf).
