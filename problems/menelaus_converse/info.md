# Converse of Menelaus's theorem

D is outside BC; E and F are inside CA and AB. Assume the magnitude product
|BD|/|DC| × |CE|/|EA| × |AF|/|FB| = 1. Prove D, E, F collinear.
The `sameclock` premises fix the directed ratio signs to (-, +, +), so this
is the directed product -1 case. No collinearity of D, E, F is a premise.
